# Build instructions

1. Use the [BOM](bom.csv) to source the motors, electronics, bearings, tubing and hardware.
2. Open the assemblies in [CAD](CAD/) in Fusion 360. Use the assembly to identify each part and its mounting points.
3. Print the plastic parts in PETG and machine the aluminum parts in 7075. Cut and drill the oval tubes to match the CAD.
4. Assemble the frame, bearings, motors and linkages. Check that each joint moves freely before fitting the covers.
5. Mount the electronics and wire them using the [schematic](Schematics/Humanoid_Electronics.pdf).
6. Check motor direction and encoder readings one joint at a time with the robot supported.

The full robot is still in development. The outer shells are unfinished.
