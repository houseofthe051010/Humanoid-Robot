## Revised dimensions and waist relief

Thigh skins grow with the 30 mm longer thigh section. Upper-arm/forearm skins follow the 275/250 mm links. Torso skins shrink 20 mm relative to the raised pelvis. Original head, hands and their shells are separate from this C27/C28 count.

Both waist halves are structural holders. The revised cuts encompass the actual hip output forks through full rotation, with 3 mm radial and axial allowance; separate flange relief covers the motor-output interface. Each result is one connected solid and has zero intersections with the conservative cutter envelopes. Front/rear source-copy volumes decrease from approximately 242.1/250.2 cm³ to 130.0/142.4 cm³. Those are geometric diagnostics, not mass or strength measurements. Minimum remaining sections, reinforcement, print orientation and load capacity need a new review.

The new elbow is a transverse double-shaft servo and attached U fork, without the previous unattached wrap/side guards. Twenty samples check the new moving fork/horns against the fixed servo case through 0° to −135°; full combined arm/torso/cable clearance is not certified.

## Retained shoes and colors

Rounded shoe uppers/rims and original contact soles remain unchanged, with the prior low vamp/open instep shape. Pale, graphite and teal appearances distinguish cosmetic skins, joint breaks and identifiers. Actual colors/material-placeholder names and bounds are read from the reopened native archive in the inventory.

V28 shoe, cuff and neck tests remain historical evidence for their original configurations. They do not establish all combinations after new torso/arm/thigh dimensions. Current native reopen verifies the saved geometry/placement, not manufacturing approval. Panel retention, printer fit, insert/tool access, polymer, strength and actual added mass remain pending.
