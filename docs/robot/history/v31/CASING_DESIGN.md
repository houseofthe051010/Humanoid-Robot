# Casings — v31

Use the root **Casings** eye to hide/show exterior covers. The 65-component grouping and 18 physical owner constraints are retained. The waist halves belong to that toggle; structural hip adapters and ankle holders remain visible with covers off.

![Waist](evidence/v31/robot_v31_waist.png)

The enlarged waist is a single continuous outer envelope split into front/rear halves, not two motor-shaped cups. Nominal 5 mm skins, a closed underside, output-end rims and retained vertical yaw mounting positions are modeled. Both sides stop at the fixed motor output boundary. Four M4 seam positions and their reference hardware remain. Internal recesses and ribs retain motor interfaces; actual clamp fit, print material and strength need qualification.

The v30 shoulder pods, torso blends, vents, cuffs, hand/head skins and knee covers remain. Two obsolete white hip-roll disks stay hidden. The central openings formerly visible through the aluminum roll-output cheeks are now blind recesses behind solid outer caps.

![Covers off](evidence/v31/robot_v31_casings_off.png)

The paired ankle motor holders have continuous straight side walls. Their existing shaft centres, crank outputs, removable motor plates and linkage interfaces remain. They are part of the structural holders rather than the cosmetic cover toggle.
